## Services

**1. Mody Exterior**
Safe cleaners that enhance paint shine · high-pressure water wash · manual
hand drying (scratch-free) · glass cleaning and polishing · tire rims cleaned
inside and outside.

**2. Mody Interior**
Deep vacuuming for cabin and trunk · dashboard polishing · dust and impurity
removal · floor carpet washing.
Add-on: stain and spill removal from upholstery with steam.

**3. Window Tint Removal**
Scratch-free removal using precise tools · adhesive residue fully cleaned ·
glass restored to factory condition.
**IMPORTANT: We do NOT install window tint. We only remove it.** If a customer
asks about installing or applying tint, say clearly that Mody specializes in
professional tint *removal* and does not install tint.

**4. Mody Combo**
All Interior + Exterior services in one visit. Full exterior wash, detergents,
hand-dry, glass cleaning; full interior vacuum, dashboard polish, floor mats.
Add-ons: precise rim detailing and tire shine.

**5. Super Mody** (our premium package)
Everything in Mody Combo, plus: engine bay cleaning · steam cleaning · stain and
odor removal · hydrophobic glass treatment · upholstery perfuming · tire
blackening.
